This is a simple lasagna recipe.

  • Onion, garlic, carrot and celery for the flavour base, a soffrito
  • Minced beef – the main protein in the dish
  • Tomato paste and canned tomatoes – for the sauce
  • Red wine – for depth of flavour
  • Lasagna sheets – the pasta component
  • Bechamel sauce – for creaminess

Now it's time to cook!

  1. Preheat the oven to 180°C.
  2. Cook the lasagna sheets according to the package instructions.
  3. Prepare the meat sauce by sautéing the vegetables and adding the minced beef, tomato paste, and red wine.
  4. Let the sauce simmer for 20 minutes.
  5. Prepare the bechamel sauce by melting butter, adding flour, and gradually whisking in milk.
  6. Assemble the lasagna by layering the sheets with the meat sauce and bechamel sauce.
  7. Bake in the preheated oven for 30-40 minutes until golden brown.

Enjoy your delicious lasagna!
